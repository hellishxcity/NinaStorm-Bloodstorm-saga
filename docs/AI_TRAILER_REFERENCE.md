# NinaStorm: The Bloodstorm — AI Trailer Reference & Visual Direction

**Purpose:** Use the nine user-provided AI trailer clips as visual inspiration for the game's tone, promotional language, and cinematic reference. These clips are concept references, not proof of in-game assets or implemented features.

## Visual language observed in the supplied clips
- A dark gothic palette dominated by black, crimson, blood-red highlights, and cold shadow.
- Nina presented as a powerful, instantly recognizable vampire queen, with dramatic red hair accents, black armor/costume silhouettes, and confident close-ups.
- Gothic castles, ruined city streets, moonlit fortresses, smoke, rain, and ominous red light.
- Short punchy title cards and weapon/ability showcase beats, suitable for social trailers and a longer reveal trailer.
- Strong contrast between Nina's character reveal, the world around her, and the red supernatural effects of her powers.

## Production guidance
1. Keep Nina's face, hair silhouette, armor details, and color palette consistent between shots. AI-generated clips may drift; treat continuity as a review requirement.
2. Separate cinematic concept footage from actual gameplay footage. Any trailer using AI concept footage should be labeled/positioned as a concept trailer unless the shots are captured from the playable build.
3. The main game target remains a mobile-first, third-person dark medieval fantasy action RPG. Keep trailers focused on medieval Veilfell rather than implying unrelated modern-city settings are playable regions unless that setting is intentionally adopted into canon.
4. Show combat readability: boss wind-ups, Nina's dodge/parry, a clear finishing blow, and a short aftermath. Avoid editing so quickly that the player cannot understand the action.
5. Use the Bloodright transformation as a signature trailer moment: boss execution → sudden hush → crimson blood infusion → weapon changes silhouette → official name card → Nina wields the transformed weapon.
6. For the first weapon-reveal teaser, use **Hammer of War → Skull-Tithe**. Later teasers can reveal **Twin Peaks → The Widow's Kiss** and **boss chain whip → Devil's Tongue**.
7. Keep text cards brief and proofread names carefully. Approved weapon names: Blood Blade, Shadow Spears, Soul Chains, The Widow's Kiss, Skull-Tithe, Devil's Tongue, and Bow of Souls. Blood Magic and Vampire Form are abilities, not handheld weapons.

## Recommended trailer structure
- **0–5 seconds — The omen:** ruined Veilfell, bell toll, blood moon or crimson storm.
- **5–12 seconds — The Queen:** a clear Nina reveal and one signature line.
- **12–22 seconds — The hunt:** fast but readable combat beats, enemy variety, and a boss telegraph.
- **22–32 seconds — The Bloodright:** execution, blood infusion, weapon transformation, title card.
- **32–40 seconds — The promise:** world shot, boss silhouettes, game title, and “Concept footage” label if shots are not captured from the game.

## Scope and truthfulness locks
- *NinaStorm: The Bloodstorm* has exactly **26** main-campaign boss slots.
- The additional **15** bosses are reserved for the sequel, *NinaStorm: Blood Rising*.
- AI trailer clips are visual references only; they do not establish that any pictured location, costume, animation, weapon, or feature is implemented.
- The Bloodright cinematic and real weapon inventory remain design targets until built and tested.
